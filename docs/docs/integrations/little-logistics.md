# ![](https://cdn.modrinth.com/data/1zZJrh9c/fdee529567ce17536fcc53177731e28304ece5af_96.webp){.mod-icon} Little Logistics

With ![](https://cdn.modrinth.com/data/1zZJrh9c/fdee529567ce17536fcc53177731e28304ece5af_96.webp){.mod-icon} [Little Logistics](https://modrinth.com/mod/little-logistics) installed, water keeps its
grade when it is filled into or taken out of its fluid tanks with a bucket.

::: warning Supported versions
NeoForge on Minecraft 1.21.1, with Little Logistics 1.21.1.1, and Forge on Minecraft 1.20.1, with
Little Logistics 1.20.1.1. Little Logistics has no build for any other loader or Minecraft version, so
they ignore it.
:::

## Fluid Tank Car and Fluid Barge

A bucket filled from a Fluid Tank Car or a Fluid Barge gets the grade of the water inside, and a bucket
poured in keeps its grade. A car of Murky water fills Murky buckets. On 1.20.1 the Fluid Hopper works
the same way.

Water already keeps its grade when it is pumped between these tanks and other mods' tanks.

How grades are worked out is on the [Water Purity](/docs/features/water-purity#the-four-grades) page.
